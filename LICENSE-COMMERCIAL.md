## Who needs a commercial licence?

AION is published under the **European Union Public Licence 1.2 (EUPL-1.2)**.
The EUPL-1.2 is a copyleft licence. You need a commercial licence if you:

- embed AION (in whole or in part) in **proprietary software** that is not
  released under a compatible open-source licence,
- distribute AION as part of a **closed-source product or service**,
- use AION in a **SaaS offering** without publishing the complete modified
  source code under EUPL-1.2 or a compatible licence,
- integrate AION into a **medical device** or a regulated software product
  and cannot comply with the EUPL-1.2 disclosure requirements,
- require a **warranty, indemnification, or support SLA** beyond what the
  open-source licence provides.

If your use case is fully compliant with EUPL-1.2 (open-source product,
internal research, academic use), **no commercial licence is required**.

A commercial licence from ISCaD GmbH grants you:

1. **Proprietary use** — the right to embed AION in closed-source software

2. **Distribution rights** — the right to distribute AION as part of a
   commercial product or service without source-code disclosure.
3. **Sublicensing** — the right to sublicence AION to your customers as part
